Research

My work sits at the intersection of collective intelligence, computational social science, and governance. I study how groups make decisions and how those mechanisms can be improved or applied to public institutions. Most recently I have become interested in wilderness search and rescue — how these volunteer organizations govern themselves and work alongside local and federal law enforcement.

Projects

Defense Industrial Base Institute

2025 – present Northeastern University

Doctoral research assistantship on an interview-based study of the defense industrial base. I conduct and code research interviews, assemble locale-specific data to inform the project, and contribute to reports and other written outputs.

Qualitative Methods Defense Policy

Pandemic Supermind & Trust CoLab

Published 2023 MIT Center for Collective Intelligence

Two large-scale collective intelligence activations applied to public health. The Pandemic Supermind convened distributed contributors to identify critical unmet needs arising from COVID-19, generating ideas across five domains: diagnostics and monitoring, viral transmission control, therapies and vaccines, communicating scientific insight, and pandemic preparedness. The companion Trust CoLab sourced scenarios for the future of public trust in healthcare and medicine.

Collective Intelligence Public Health Governance

Supermind Design in Professional Services

Published 2023 MIT Center for Collective Intelligence

A field application of Supermind Design — a method for configuring new combinations of people and computers that are more collectively intelligent than their predecessors — carried out with professionals at Deloitte. The work tested whether a structured design approach could introduce fresh thinking and creative problem-solving into an established organization.

Organizational Design Human–Computer Groups

AI for Leadership

Published 2023 MIT Open Learning

Implementation and evaluation of an AI education program built for organizational leaders, developed and assessed with colleagues across MIT Open Learning and the MIT Media Lab.
